Eye Care is a free, open-source macOS menu bar app for eye breaks and eye-drop reminders, with a monochrome gradient theme and local activity history.

## Features

- A 20-minute focus timer and guided 20-second rest.
- A completion chime, enabled by default, with a separate setting from notification sounds.
- Daily eye-drop reminders, one-click logging, undo, and seven-day history.
- Menu bar indicators for overdue breaks and eye drops.
- Automatic pause during sleep or screen lock, optional countdown in the menu bar, and optional launch at login.
- No account, tracking, or app-initiated network requests. Settings and activity stay on your Mac.

## Requirements and installation

macOS 14 or later. The release package is designed as a universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel.

After the verified download is available, extract the ZIP, move **Eye Care.app** to Applications, and open it. Click the eye icon in the menu bar; there is no Dock icon. Enable notifications and configure daily reminders in Settings.

## Notes

- macOS notification and Focus settings control reminder delivery.
- A guided rest must finish to count as a completed break.
- Every app launch starts a new 20-minute focus timer.
- Eye-drop reminders use the times you configure and do not specify dosage.
